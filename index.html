What we clean

Exterior cleaning that protects your investment

As a property owner, Devante understands how quickly Florida humidity turns clean surfaces into dirt, mildew, rust and oil. These are the services that put your property back to new.

01

House & Soft Washing

Low-pressure soft washing lifts green mildew and road film off stucco, siding and painted surfaces without forcing water where it doesn’t belong.

  • Stucco & vinyl siding
  • Mildew & algae removal
  • Landscape safe rinse
02

Driveway & Concrete Cleaning

Commercial surface cleaners pull years of grey out of driveways, garage aprons and pool decks — edge to edge, with no zebra striping.

  • Driveways & aprons
  • Pool decks & patios
  • Even, streak-free finish
03

Gutter Cleaning

Pine needles and packed debris get cleared out so water actually moves — then the gutter faces get washed so the whole roofline matches.

  • Debris removal
  • Downspout flow check
  • Exterior gutter brightening
04

Soffit, Fascia & Roofline

The dirtiest part of the house is the part you look up at. We wash soffits, fascia and eaves back to their original bright white.

  • Soffit & fascia wash
  • Eave & overhang detail
  • Spider web & nest removal
05

Sidewalk & Walkway Cleaning

Curb appeal starts at the street. Sidewalks, entry paths and front steps get the same surface-cleaner treatment as the driveway.

  • Sidewalks & entry paths
  • Front steps & landings
  • Slip-causing algae removed
06

Rust, Oil & Mildew Removal

Irrigation rust, dropped oil and stubborn organic staining treated with the right chemistry instead of brute force that scars concrete.

  • Sprinkler rust staining
  • Oil & tire marks
  • Mildew & organic growth
