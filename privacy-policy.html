Privacy Policy

How Pro Vets Pressure Washing LLC handles information when you visit this website or contact us about a free estimate.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Pro Vets Pressure Washing LLC (“Pro Vets Pressure Washing,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may receive, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the exterior cleaning services we provide across St. Johns, Mandarin, St. Augustine, Ponte Vedra, Nocatee, Jax Beach and surrounding areas in Florida.

Information we collect

This website does not include contact forms and does not ask you to create an account, so we do not collect personal information through the site itself. We may receive information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly. When you call or text the number listed on this site, we receive your phone number and whatever details you choose to share — such as your name, property address, and the services you are interested in.
  • Service records. If you book work with us, we keep basic records needed to schedule jobs, perform the work, and provide estimates, invoices and receipts.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the date and time of your visit. This is used for security and to keep the site running properly.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Respond to your call or text and provide a free estimate;
  • Schedule, perform and follow up on pressure washing and exterior cleaning services;
  • Send job-related communications such as appointment confirmations, invoices and receipts;
  • Maintain business records and comply with legal, tax and insurance obligations;
  • Protect the security and proper operation of this website.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, our website host or a payment or invoicing provider), and only to the extent needed to perform that work. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Text messages and calls

If you call or text us, standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. We use your phone number to respond to your inquiry and coordinate service. You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, and we will honor that request.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Images and assets may be served from third-party content delivery networks, which may log standard request data as part of delivering those files. You can control cookies and similar technologies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep customer and job records only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect the information in our possession, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

Our services and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You may request that we correct or delete the information we hold about you, or that we stop contacting you, by calling or texting (904) 533-6762. We will respond to reasonable requests as required by applicable law.

Third-party links

This site links to third-party platforms such as Facebook and TikTok. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites — please review their policies directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Call or text Pro Vets Pressure Washing LLC at (904) 533-6762. We serve St. Johns County and the Jacksonville beaches in Florida.
